Roofing questions, answered.

Straight answers to the questions Long Island homeowners ask us most.

01How much does a new roof cost on Long Island?

It depends on the size and pitch of your roof, the materials you choose, and the condition of what's there now. Because every home is different, the only accurate number is a free written estimate. We'll give you a clear, no-pressure price for your home.

02How long does a roof last on Long Island?

A quality asphalt shingle roof typically lasts 20 to 30 years, though coastal weather, sun and storms can shorten that. Architectural and premium shingles last longer. If your roof is 20 or more years old or showing wear, it's a smart time for a free estimate.

03Do I need a repair or a full replacement?

An isolated leak, a few missing shingles or minor storm damage usually just needs a repair. If your roof is aging, damaged across wide areas or keeps leaking, a replacement is often more cost-effective long term. We tell you honestly which makes sense and never upsell work you don't need.

04Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. FJR Affordable Roofing is fully licensed and insured, including roofing insurance, for every job across Nassau and Suffolk County.

05Do you offer free estimates?

Always. Every estimate is free, written and no-pressure. We look at your roof, explain what we find, and give you a clear quote so you can decide what's right for your home and budget.

06Do you handle emergency leaks and storm damage?

Yes. If a storm damaged your roof or you have an active leak, call (516) 805-0400 right away and we'll get to you quickly to stop further damage.
